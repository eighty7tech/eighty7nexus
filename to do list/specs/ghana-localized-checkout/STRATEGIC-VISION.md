## Risk Management & Mitigation

### Technology Risks

**Risk: Scalability issues during growth**
- Mitigation: Cloud-native architecture, horizontal scaling, load testing
- Contingency: Managed services (Firebase, AWS) as backup

**Risk: GPS/Network reliability**
- Mitigation: Offline-first architecture, SMS fallback, redundant systems
- Contingency: Manual tracking as fallback

**Risk: Payment gateway failures**
- Mitigation: Multiple payment providers, retry logic, wallet system
- Contingency: Cash on delivery option

### Operational Risks

**Risk: Driver adoption slower than expected**
- Mitigation: Competitive pay, gamification, training, incentives
- Contingency: Partnerships with existing logistics companies

**Risk: Merchant adoption challenges**
- Mitigation: White-glove onboarding, free trial period, support team
- Contingency: Direct sales and B2B partnerships

**Risk: Regulatory changes**
- Mitigation: Legal team, government relations, compliance monitoring
- Contingency: Flexible business model adaptable to regulations

### Market Risks

**Risk: Competitor entry (e.g., global player entering Ghana)**
- Mitigation: Network effects, local expertise, superior product
- Contingency: Strategic partnerships, acquisition opportunities

**Risk: Economic downturn reducing e-commerce**
- Mitigation: B2B/B2C2B models, essential goods focus, cost reduction
- Contingency: Pivot to general logistics market

**Risk: Key talent departure**
- Mitigation: Competitive compensation, equity incentives, culture
- Contingency: Cross-training, documentation, knowledge sharing
